Your data stays on your device

All courses you upload are stored in your browser's IndexedDB — a local database built into your browser. Nothing is ever sent to a server.

Your courses will persist across page reloads, but will be permanently deleted if you:

  • Clear your browser's site data or cookies
  • Use "Clear browsing data" in your browser settings
  • Browse in private / incognito mode

To back up your courses, keep a copy of your original .pgn files.
